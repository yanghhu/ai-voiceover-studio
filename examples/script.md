[NARRATOR]: Look up on a clear day and the sky is blue. But sunlight is white. So where does the blue come from?

[ALEX](tags=curious): Wait — sunlight is white? It looks yellow to me.

[NARRATOR]: Sunlight contains every colour of the rainbow.
When it enters the atmosphere, it bumps into tiny gas molecules.
Short, blue wavelengths scatter much more than long, red ones.

[ALEX](speed=1.05): So blue light bounces all over the sky, and that's what we see from every direction!

[NARRATOR]: Exactly. And at sunset, light travels through more air, so the blue is scattered away — leaving **reds and oranges**.

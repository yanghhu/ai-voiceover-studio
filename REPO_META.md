## Description (≤ 350 characters)

Open-source AI voiceover & faceless video generator (Python CLI). Turn a script into text-to-speech narration, SRT subtitles and YouTube, Shorts or TikTok videos with word-by-word captions, B-roll and ducked music. Free Edge voices, no API key; OpenAI and Miragea voice cloning optional. Agent-ready.
